# wayfinder — vision

## The scarce resource is attention

A team member sits down with Amplifier already knowing what they came to do.
The tool can do far more than they know — workflows, commands, whole bundles a
colleague found last week — but every one of those is invisible until someone
tells them, at the moment it matters, in a way that costs almost nothing to
absorb. The gap wayfinder closes is **awareness, not capability.** The
capabilities already exist; almost nobody knows the ones they don't already use.

So the thing wayfinder protects, above correctness, above coverage, above
feature count, is the reader's attention. A good wayfinder moment leaves the
user knowing one true, useful thing — or having been offered one — and it cost
them a glance. That is the whole bar.

## What wayfinder is

**One curated, opinionated voice** telling a colleague what's worth their
attention right now. Not a feed, not a changelog, not marketing. Someone has
used this system daily and is pointing at the single thing worth trying — and
then getting out of the way.

Concretely, two moves:

1. **Surface what's worth knowing** — an authored bulletin, a practice, a
   concept — led by the one item most worth the reader's time, not an
   exhaustive list.
2. **Distinguish requests from offers** — a direct request authorizes ordinary
   in-scope work without another Wayfinder ack. When Wayfinder introduces an
   optional next step, **show the exact action first** and act only on an
   explicit "sure." Native host, tool, safety, and destructive-action approvals
   still apply.

## What wayfinder is not

- **Not an absorber.** wayfinder *points*; it never re-implements another
  domain's expertise. The capabilities live where they live — skills load by
  name, app-CLI questions route to the CLI expert, concepts get a thin note and
  a pointer to the real source. wayfinder owns the *communication* about what's
  new and possible, nothing more.
- **Not a feed.** One thing at a time. Curated means someone chose. Rotation and
  seen-memory keep it from repeating itself; decline-memory lets a "no" stick.
- **Not autonomous.** A signal match authorizes reading the relevant packet,
  not executing its actions. An explicit user request to run or install
  something authorizes that in-scope work without duplicate Wayfinder ack;
  native approvals still apply. Only Wayfinder-initiated optional actions and
  installs use propose → show → ack → act. A packet never acts merely because
  its topic matched.
- **Not stale.** Content is mined from *current* practice and refreshed often.
  Before repeating a claim, prefer what's verified now and point at the source
  of truth — commands are verified at authoring time so a packet always carries
  runnable actions.

## Why authored, not generated

A neutral feed averages everything to the same weight and drowns the reader in
it — which spends the exact resource wayfinder exists to protect. An authored
voice is a filter with a point of view: it can say "this one, not those twelve,"
be honest that something is early or "bought with a real failure," and name a
tradeoff plainly instead of hedging. The opinion *is* the value. wayfinder is
authored on purpose, and stays authored as it grows.

## The measure of success

wayfinder is working when a team member's good workflow reaches the next person
**without the author having to repeat themselves** — when the thing one person
discovered shows up, at the right moment, in the next person's session, and
costs them a glance to adopt. Propagation of practice, paid in attention as
cheaply as possible. That is the north star; everything mechanical serves it.

## How it grows — the ring model

wayfinder widens only as each ring proves out, optimizing for out-sooner and
improve-in-the-wild:

- **Ring 0** — one person, daily-driven: the thinnest end-to-end thing that works.
- **Ring 1** — a small group of observed testers; deterministic firing and
  enforced decline-memory earn their place here.
- **Ring 2** — public: team distribution and a public bundle, timed off observed
  signal, not a calendar.

Each ring is a chance to learn in real use before widening — the same discipline
the product itself embodies: surface the one thing worth knowing, prove it in
the world, and only then reach further.
